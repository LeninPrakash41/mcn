# Starter packs by industry

Where to start for common kinds of business, combining a template, connectors and the parts of MCN that matter most there. Open the template from **Templates** in Studio, connect your systems in the **Connect** tab, and change the rest with the Agent.

| Industry | Start from | Connect | Add |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clinics and healthcare** | Voice Agent Builder (a phone receptionist), Customer CRM & Lead Engine (patients and appointments) | WhatsApp reminders, Google Sheets | `use "healthcare"`, company sign-in and `audit` services |
| **Sales teams** | Customer CRM & Lead Engine, Sales Intelligence Hub | HubSpot or Salesforce, Slack | An AI assistant with `agent_run` that looks up deals |
| **Retail and inventory** | Inventory & Item Manager, Operations Dashboard | Excel and CSV imports, Google Sheets, Slack alerts | A phone app for the shop floor (`navbar "floating"`) |
| **Field services and construction** | Construction Project Manager, Field Inspection & Site Audit (phone) | WhatsApp updates to customers, Google Sheets | Photos and checklists on phones, IoT sensors on site |
| **Operations and back office** | Operations Dashboard, Workflow Automation, Executive Analytics & BI Hub | A SQL database (`use "sql"`), Slack | Approvals with `checkpoint`, scheduled reports with `schedule` |
| **Customer support** | AI Agent Builder, Voice Agent Builder | Slack or WhatsApp, HubSpot tickets | A `chat_widget` on your site |
| **Agriculture and IoT** | Greenhouse Lab, Smart Home IoT Controller, IoT Telemetry Ingestion Hub | MQTT devices, Slack alerts | [Control policies](#/guide/iot) and an emergency stop |
| **Data and AI teams** | Data Labeling Studio, ML Training Lab | Google Sheets, a SQL database | `classify` and `extract` to pre-label, `audit_log` for review |
| **Education** | The student templates, Greenhouse Lab and Rover Lab | — | The [student pilot kit](#/getting-started/student-pilot) and the [Developer Club](#/guide/developer-club) |

Every template runs, builds to a web app and a phone app, and has tests, so you start from something that works. When you need a hand building it, the Developer Club's **Hire a builder** page lists members open to work.
